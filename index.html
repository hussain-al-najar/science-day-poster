2Netflix Prize error

Netflix's own system (Cinematch) had a test error of RMSE 0.9525. The winning team in 2009 reached 0.8567.

(0.9525 − 0.8567) / 0.9525 = 0.1006 → 10.06% improvement

RMSE (root mean squared error) measures how far predicted ratings are from real ratings, so lower is better.
